# Sources & References

## Ancient Greek Sources

### Primary Texts
- Homer (~8th century BCE). *The Iliad*. Early Greek epic containing references to monsters.
- Hesiod (~8th century BCE). *Theogony*. Genealogy of the gods and monsters, including Hydra's parentage.
- Apollodorus (~2nd century CE). *Bibliotheca* (Library). Comprehensive collection of Greek myths including detailed account of Hercules' labors.
- Diodorus Siculus (1st century BCE). *Library of History*. Historical work including mythological accounts.

### Roman Sources
- Ovid (1st century BCE-1st century CE). *Metamorphoses*. Roman retelling of Greek myths.
- Hyginus (1st century CE). *Fabulae*. Collection of Greek and Roman myths.
